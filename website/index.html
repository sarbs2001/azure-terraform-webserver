Smoke Shack

Proudly Serving Calaveras County for Over 12 Years

For more than 12 years, our family-owned smoke shop has been proud to serve the Calaveras County community. We welcome both our local customers and visitors passing through the area. We believe great service is just as important as a great selection. That's why we strive to provide a friendly, welcoming experience every time you stop by. Whether you're looking for your usual favorites or something new, we're always happy to help. Our shop carries a wide variety of products, including cigarettes, pipe tobacco, cigars, tobacco pipes, glassware, beverages, snacks, clothing, jewelry, and more. Stop by and see us next time you're in Valley Springs! We are located at 200 CA-12 Suite C4, Valley Springs, CA 95252 in the Valley Oaks Center.
